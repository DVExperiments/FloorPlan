About Floorplan Studio

A friendly place to sketch, experiment and explore house ideas.

Design without the complexity

Floorplan Studio is a free-form conceptual home-design playground. Create accurate-to-scale 2D layouts, add furniture and site features, compare heating and lighting scenarios, then explore the result in a simplified interactive 3D dollhouse. No account or installation needed.

Privacy & projects

Projects are stored in this browser on this device; they are not automatically synced online. Clearing site data may remove them. Export JSON copies regularly, especially before changing devices or website addresses.

Concept designs only

Thermal and lighting estimates are illustrative. Dimensions, wall thicknesses and roof forms do not establish structural safety or New Zealand Building Code compliance. Consult qualified professionals for real construction.

0° = north points up on the grid; 90° = north points right. Rotating north changes the home's real-world orientation without moving rooms.

Window-specific glazing, exterior shades and eaves can be changed by selecting a window. Solar position uses an approximate NZ solar-clock calculation; irradiance and shadows are clear-sky approximations adjusted by cloud cover, not observed weather.

Building & weather assumptions

Outdoor °C is the daily mean; the optional swing peaks around 3pm and is lowest around 3am. Heat pumps lose capacity and COP in colder air. Solar gain is calculated from window direction, sun altitude, glazing and simplified shading.

Shortcut guide?

Exact size: expand Precision Drawing, enter the dimensions, then click a start corner with Rectangle selected. Measure: press M and click two points. Layers: use visibility and lock controls to organise the plan. Multi-select: Drag a selection box from empty canvas, or Shift-click objects; drag any selected room to move the whole group. 4 opens Site & Garden.  Furniture and heater arrows rotate around their centre. Rooms rotate in quarter-turns, preserving wall-attached openings. Doors and windows stay attached to their walls; door arrows change their hinge or swing, and window arrows slide them along the wall. Heater direction is visual only and does not yet change simulated heat flow. Shortcuts are disabled while typing in fields.
